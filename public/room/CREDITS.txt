ATTIC ROOM — REVISION 6 — SOURCE ATTRIBUTION

REVISION 6 — COHESIVE MATERIAL AND FIDELITY PASS

Existing furniture, room layout and game-cover artwork are retained. The desktop
monitor, keyboard and mouse are rebuilt; the other device models are retained.
The floor pillow, ceiling beams and wall posters/cards are removed; the rug
is replaced with the supplied model credited below. The original lightweight
basket and papers remain; the basket clears the desk and papers settle on its base.
Attribution notes below
continue to apply. No Bruno Simon geometry, textures or code are redistributed.

Visual/material reference: Bruno Simon, My Room in 3D
https://my-room-in-3d.vercel.app/
https://github.com/brunosimon/my-room-in-3d
Studied its rounded silhouettes, quiet materials, coherent baked illumination,
contact shadows and warm/cool light separation.

Original modifications in this revision: unified cream enamel, muted teal cloth,
oiled oak/walnut, slate ABS and brushed-metal finishes; restrained procedural
grain; bevelled floorboards within the original floor footprint; refined furniture
edges and welded/smoothed chair meshes. Labels on retained devices and original
screens are retained. The TV's original color, normal and roughness/metallic maps
are restored without the matte/normal-strength overrides; other devices retain
the restrained surface treatment.
Static environment and ALL four devices now share one Cycles lighting setup.
The original night-light colors and localized tungsten/amber practicals are retained.
Broad blue fills and world illumination are substantially reduced for deeper shadows
and stronger contrast around the desk, lava lamp and narrow window highlights.
The four desk drawer fronts now share the desk's cream enamel. The old rug/fringe
is replaced with the supplied folded rug model credited below, squared to the old
2.3514 m width, rotated 15 degrees clockwise with the folded corner nearest the TV.
The floor keeps its warm oak color with more natural grain from the original CC0 scan.
The rug's new geometric textile
texture was generated with OpenAI image generation from the user's visual reference.
The runtime uses baked, unlit materials, with the existing interactive CRT screen
and animated lava preserved. Recipe: tools/room/restyle.py and tools/room/bake.py.
