ATTIC ROOM — CREDITS

The room is made in the browser: its geometry is generated by code (src/room/gen),
its surfaces are functions, its light is baked on the visitor's GPU, and its
posters, prints, game covers and the computer's wallpaper are original designs
painted by code. No third-party model, texture or image file is shipped with it.
