ROEL.WORK — CREDITS

Roel Leenders' portfolio: a 16-bit arcade game on a CRT television in a 3D
attic room. Design, code, game, pixel art and music by Roel Leenders unless
noted below.

THE ROOM

The room is original work, made in the visitor's browser. Its geometry is
generated by code, its surfaces are functions, its light is baked on the
visitor's GPU, and its posters, game covers, device legends and the computer's
wallpaper are fictional designs painted by code for this site. No third-party
model, texture, photograph or artwork is shipped with it, and the wordmarks on
its devices are the site's own.

An earlier, unpublished version of the room was assembled from sourced models.
None of their meshes, UV layouts or textures is included here.

Visual reference for the room's style:
Bruno Simon, My Room in 3D — https://my-room-in-3d.vercel.app/
